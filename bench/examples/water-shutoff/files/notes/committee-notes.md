## Water shutoff

- Brightwater Plumbing replaces the main valve in the basement on Wednesday
  18 November. Water goes off for the whole building at 9:00.
- Hot water too: the boiler has to be switched off while the valve is out.
  Heating is on a separate circuit and stays on.
- End time: their foreman said "mid-afternoon, probably by three, but it
  depends on what we find once the old valve is out." Not confirmed. Jonas
  will post on the noticeboard when the water is back.
- Why: the old valve leaked in October and flooded the basement storage. The
  insurer wants it replaced before they renew the policy in December.
- Tenants don't need to do anything to their own taps or pipes.
- Ana's tips: fill a few bottles and the kettle the night before, keep a
  bucket of water for flushing, don't start the washing machine or the
  dishwasher that morning.
- When the water comes back, it may run brown or cloudy for a minute. Run the
  cold tap in the kitchen until it's clear. The pipes may bang for a while
  because of air in them.
- Anyone who needs water that day for medical reasons, or who can't carry
  water, should tell Jonas by Friday 13 November. Ana can bring water up.
- Basement storage stays locked all day. Don't leave anything in the corridor
  down there.
