**Water shutoff on Tuesday 18 November**

Dear neighbours,

On Tuesday 18 November, Brightwater Plumbing will replace the main water valve in the basement. The water will be off for the whole building from 9:00.

**What will be off**

- Cold water and hot water will both be off. The boiler has to be switched off while the old valve is out.
- The heating runs on a separate circuit, so it will stay on.

**When the water comes back**

The plumbers' foreman expects to finish by mid-afternoon, probably around 3:00. He can't promise that time, because it depends on what they find once the old valve is out. I'll put a notice on the noticeboard as soon as the water is back on.

**Why it's being done**

In October the old valve leaked and flooded the basement storage. Our insurer wants the valve replaced before the building's policy is renewed in December.

**What to do**

You don't need to do anything to your own taps or pipes. Ana from the committee suggests a few things to make the day easier:

- The night before, fill a few bottles and your kettle.
- Keep a bucket of water ready for flushing the toilet.
- Don't start the washing machine or the dishwasher that morning.

When the water comes back on, it may run brown or cloudy for a minute. Run the cold kitchen tap until the water is clear. The pipes may also bang for a while, because there is air in them. Both are normal.

**Basement storage**

The basement storage will stay locked all day. Please don't leave anything in the basement corridor.

**If you need help**

If you need water that day for medical reasons, or you can't carry water yourself, please tell me by Friday 14 November. Ana can bring water up to your flat.
